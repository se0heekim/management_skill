Background and purpose

This report analyzes 48 months of search volume for keywords searched together with 'magnesium supplement' to show when searches rise each year in the United States (Google) market and which searches have recently grown or shrunk. Keywords were collected from ListeningMind related-search and co-search data, and monthly search volume was used to identify seasonal keywords that repeat every year, last-12-month trends, and last-3-month risers and fallers.

Selection criteria
STEP 12-month season window

Two consecutive months count as one window, so a peak that shifts by a month from year to year still counts as the same season.

In at least 3 of the 4 years, the month with the most searches falls in that window.

The seasonal index (that month's searches ÷ that year's monthly average, median over 4 years) is 1.4 or higher. Keywords that pass are listed by their average volume in that month.
